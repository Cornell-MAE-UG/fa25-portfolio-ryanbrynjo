## Overview

For this portfolio assignment, I selected **Problem Set #4 from MAE 3240 (Heat Transfer)** because it was one of the most valuable assignments for strengthening my understanding of practical thermal analysis. The assignment combined several key heat transfer concepts into realistic engineering problems: modeling a photovoltaic panel using a thermal resistance network, analyzing a rod acting as an extended surface with an insulated tip, and evaluating the performance of a square pin fin with a convection tip. Working through these problems required identifying the governing equations, applying appropriate boundary conditions, and understanding the physical assumptions that allow complex heat transfer systems to be simplified into solvable models.

The photovoltaic panel problem was particularly valuable because it linked heat transfer directly to system performance. The silicon temperature influenced the solar-to-electric conversion efficiency, meaning the thermal model affected the electrical output of the system. This highlighted how thermal management plays a critical role in real engineering designs. The fin problems reinforced how geometry, material properties, and convection conditions influence temperature distributions and heat dissipation. These ideas are directly applicable to many mechanical and aerospace systems where maintaining safe operating temperatures is essential, such as propulsion systems, electronics cooling, and energy systems.
