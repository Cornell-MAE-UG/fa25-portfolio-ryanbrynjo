During the summer of 2025, my friend and I, Kyan Barth (Engineer at University of North Dakota) decided to make a go-kart.

Our main objectives for building a go-kart was learning how to weld, how to design a project, and how to fabricate scrap metal materials.

![Shaded rendering of earlier version]({{ "/assets/images/radio-machine.jpg" | relative_url }}){: .inline-image-r style="width: 200px"}

We started with documentation and early CAD designs.
