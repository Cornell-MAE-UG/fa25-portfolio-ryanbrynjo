During the summer of 2025, my friend and I, Kyan Barth (Engineer at University of North Dakota) decided to make a go-kart.

Our main objectives for building a go-kart was learning how to weld, how to design a project, how to utilize CAD, and how to fabricate scrap metal materials.

![Photo of me welding frame]({{ "/assets/images/IMG_8811" | relative_url }}){: .inline-image-r style="width: 200px"}

We started with documentation and early CAD designs. We set a budget of 700 dollars and allocated this budget to specific parts of the go-kart (engine, chassis, frame, etc.)

![Photo of initial CAD Design]({{ "/assets/images/IMG_8811.png" | relative_url }}){: .inline-image-r style="width: 200px"}
